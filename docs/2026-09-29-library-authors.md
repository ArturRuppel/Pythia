# Author bylines in the reading app

Paper and stub rows show the byline beneath the title, including the shared
rows in Reading. Up to six authors are shown in full. Longer lists show the
first two and last two in source order, with a native disclosure to show all
names and collapse again. Missing authors produce no empty line.

The paper title and key open the paper; the author disclosure operates in place
and is keyboard accessible. It is separate from the navigation button to avoid
nested interactive controls. No journal line is added: the citekey already
identifies the venue.
